### Claude:

The Pasta Generator now rolls a random sauce for every shape, and each shape's parameter table has a dedicated Sauce dropdown. All 182 unit tests pass and datagen regenerated the lang file. Nothing is committed.

**What changed**

- **New sauce parameter.** [PastaParam.java](src/main/java/us/drullk/noodling/noodler/PastaParam.java) gains a `sauce` name, a `SAUCE` kind, and a factory that every shape declares right after its pasta block. `ResolvedParams.sauce()` returns the chosen state, where air means no sauce.
- **Odds.** [Util.java](src/main/java/us/drullk/noodling/util/Util.java) rolls from a weighted list: air at weight 3 and each of Alfredo, Cheddar and Tomato at weight 1, which gives exactly 1/2 and 1/6 each.
- **Dropdown.** [NoodlerPanel.java](src/main/java/us/drullk/noodling/client/noodler/NoodlerPanel.java) draws a separate combo for the sauce kind with "No sauce" first, then the three sauce blocks. It has its own seed field and lock like every other row, so a sauce can be pinned or reseeded independently.
- **Placers.** All six placers declare the parameter and pass `params.sauce()` instead of the hardcoded cheddar or air from the sauce commit. I also made the loop skip the write above the pasta when the sauce is air. Without that, "no sauce" wrote explicit air blocks into exported structure templates, which paste as air rather than structure void.
- **Lang.** The provider adds "No sauce", and the regenerated [en_us.json](src/generated/resources/assets/noodling/lang/en_us.json) now has that entry plus the auto-derived "Sauce" parameter label.
- **Tests.** [PastaShapesTest.java](src/test/java/us/drullk/noodling/noodler/PastaShapesTest.java) checks every shape declares a sauce of the right kind, that rolls are air or a sauce block, and that over 6000 rolls the counts land near 3000 air and 1000 per sauce.

Existing Noodler states saved before this change simply lack a sauce entry, so they roll one from the Noodler seed like any unlocked parameter. Every modified class cites the transcript path for this session; the transcript itself is generated only when you ask.
