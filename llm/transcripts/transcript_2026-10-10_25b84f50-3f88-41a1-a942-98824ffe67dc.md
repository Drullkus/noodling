**Claude:**

The Noodler and its ImGui settings screen are implemented, built, unit-tested and verified in-game. Nothing is committed yet; all changes sit in the working tree.

**What changed in the platform picture.** Minecraft 26.3 no longer uses GLFW or raw OpenGL. Windowing is SDL3 and drawing goes through a Vulkan-first GPU abstraction with OpenGL fallback. The reference repo's two ImGui backends and its mixins therefore could not be ported. Instead, imgui-java 1.92.7.1 (universal macOS native, built for the same LWJGL 3.4.1 the game ships) is bundled through Jar-in-Jar, input is fed from a vanilla `Screen`, and draw data is rendered through Minecraft's own pipeline API. No mixins were added.

**Item behaviour, per your choices.**
- Right-click (not sneaking, main hand) opens the ImGui panel over the vanilla blurred, darkened background; Esc closes it. Sneak-right-click passes through to blocks.
- Holding left click for 25 ticks and releasing places a shape at the first block, fluid or entity along the view line up to 128 blocks, or at the end of the line on a miss. Early release cancels. Left click never attacks or mines while the Noodler is held. The crossbow shoot sound plays.
- First-person bow draw transform, third-person bow-and-arrow arm pose and the bow FOV zoom are driven by a charge marker component, so no vanilla "using item" state is needed.
- Seeds follow the world-creation screen rules. When unlocked, each placement rolls a fresh Noodler seed, uses it, and writes it back so the seed shown is the one that made the shape you see. Every parameter gets a seed derived from it; a locked parameter rolls from its own seed, and editing a knob clears that seed. Typing a seed into a parameter row locks it. Slider bounds recompute live from the parameters above.
- Edits apply locally at once and are sent to the server as one whole-state payload when a widget finishes its edit; the server only writes to a Noodler in the sender's main hand.

**Code layout.** [NoodlerItem.java](src/main/java/us/drullk/noodling/noodler/NoodlerItem.java) plus state, descriptors and placement in `us.drullk.noodling.noodler`; payloads in `us.drullk.noodling.network`; the ImGui context, SDL-scancode key map and GPU renderer in `us.drullk.noodling.client.imgui`; the screen, panel, charge tracker and item extensions in `us.drullk.noodling.client.noodler`. Each placer gained a `shape()` descriptor and its `placeRandom*` now delegates to it with the same roll order, so the commands behave as before. The item is first in the creative tab, Epic, unstackable, with the wheat pasta sprite and a generated name.
